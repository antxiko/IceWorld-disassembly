Fifty mazes of ice: the hammer that breaks blocks, the L-shaped pieces put together in pairs, the eggs that crack and the creatures that look for the hero. The ten keywords, the bonus stage every five and the end with its kanji; the title from the ROM's bytes, checked against openMSX; the game in GIF and the five tunes from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1985 - 16 KB cartridge at 0x8000 - MSX - Dump 167b226b...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The logo and the title come from running the ROM (checked against openMSX), and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the title, the KEYWORD, the controls, the hammer, TIME, the lives and the points.
  • The stages: the maps, the pieces, the eggs and the creatures, the bonus stage and the end, in GIF.
  • The sprites: the hero, the hammer and the two creatures put together from the ROM tables, frame by frame.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM and the ten keywords.
  • The music: the five tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: stage 00 after stage 9, the keyword of the bonus, maps among the drawings and the code nobody reaches.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the BIOS it uses, the interrupt and the loop, the RAM and the tools.

This is documentation and preservation work: the code and artwork still belong to their authors and to Casio, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
